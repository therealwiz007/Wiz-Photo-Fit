Wiz Photo Fit – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Developer: Wiz Labs

Wiz Photo Fit resizes photos and signatures to exact sizes for exam forms, ID cards and passports, and compresses images. Everything happens on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. Your photos are never uploaded: all resizing happens on your phone.

Your photos

  • You choose photos with Android's own photo picker or camera. The app only sees the photos you pick.
  • Edited files are saved to your phone's Pictures/Wiz Photo Fit folder, or wherever you choose in the save dialog.
  • Face detection and background change use Google ML Kit models. Google Play services downloads these models once, and the app then runs them on your phone. The app itself has no internet access. The model download is handled by Google Play services under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Temporary working copies stay in the app's private cache and are deleted by Android when space is needed.

Presets

Exam size presets are compiled from public exam notifications and stored inside the app. Presets you make stay on your phone unless you share a preset code yourself. The app is not affiliated with any government body or exam authority; always check your latest official notification.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission: internet, used only by Google Play to check for and download app updates. The app itself does not send your photos or any data anywhere.

Ads

This version shows no ads. Future versions may show ads, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
