Wiz Photo Fit is a photo and signature resizer for online forms. It sets the exact size in pixels and the exact KB that the form asks for: SSC, UPSC, RRB railway, IBPS / SBI bank, NEET, JEE, CUET, GATE, state PSC, police and more. Pick your exam, pick a photo, and get a file that fits the limit, like 20–50 KB photo and 10–20 KB signature. Search your exam, crop with guides, and see a pass/fail check for size, KB and format before you save. Snip, a small helper, reacts as you search and check. Resizing runs on your phone. Your photos never leave it.
